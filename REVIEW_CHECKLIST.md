# Review Checklist

For each day, please check:

- [ ] **Name and spelling** of the Goddess's form (Hindi and English transliteration)
- [ ] **Story / katha** matches our tradition
- [ ] **Bhog (offering)** matches our community's practice
- [ ] **Mantra** and the Sanskrit **dhyana shloka** are correct (verify against a printed source or a pandit)
- [ ] **Chakra associations** are acceptable to include (these are tradition-based, not in every text)
- [ ] **Tone**: respectful and suitable for all age groups in the group
- [ ] **Length**: roughly 2-3 minutes to read
- [ ] **Hindi and English** versions say the same thing
- [ ] **Image** chosen is respectful, clear, and free to use

## Known points to confirm

- Dhyana shlokas were written from memory of commonly recited verses; please verify word by word.
- Kanya Pujan is described on Day 8 (Ashtami) and Day 9 (Navami); some families do it on only one of these days.
- Bhog items and Kalaratri/Mahagauri details vary by region.

🌺 *Navratri Day 1: Maa Shailputri* 🌺

_"Ya Devi Sarvabhuteshu Shakti-Rupena Samsthita,_
_Namastasyai Namastasyai Namastasyai Namo Namah."_

🙏 *Jai Mata Di!* Today is the first day of Shardiya Navratri. With Kalash Sthapana (Ghatasthapana), we begin the worship of the first form of Maa Durga, *Maa Shailputri*.

*🏔️ Meaning of the name*
"Shaila" means mountain and "Putri" means daughter. She is the daughter of Himalaya, the king of mountains, hence the name Shailputri.

*📖 The story*
In her previous birth she was *Sati*, daughter of Prajapati Daksha. Unable to bear the insult to her husband Lord Shiva at Daksha's yagna, Sati gave herself to the sacrificial fire. In her next birth she was born to Himalaya and became known as Shailputri. She is also called *Parvati* and *Hemavati*.

*🪷 Her form*
• A trident in the right hand, a lotus in the left
• A crescent moon on her forehead
• Her vehicle is the bull (Vrishabha), so she is also called "Vrisharudha"

*🧘 Spiritual significance*
It is believed that worshipping her steadies the seeker's mind at the *Muladhara Chakra*, the foundation of spiritual practice. Just as a mountain stays firm, the Goddess gives us steadiness and patience in life.

*🍚 Bhog (offering)*
Pure *cow's ghee* or things made from it.

*🕉️ Mantra*
_Om Devi Shailaputryai Namah_
(can be chanted 108 times during puja)

*✨ Message of the day*
Every great undertaking begins with a strong foundation. Take one small resolution today: a daily prayer, giving up a bad habit, or helping someone. Keep it for all nine days.

May Maa Shailputri bless your home with happiness, peace and stability. 🙏

*Jai Mata Di!* 🚩

🌺 *Navratri Day 9: Maa Siddhidatri (Maha Navami)* 🌺

_"Siddhagandharvayakshadyairasurairamarairapi,_
_Sevyamana Sada Bhuyat Siddhida Siddhidayini."_

🙏 *Jai Mata Di!* Today is the ninth and final day of Navratri, *Maha Navami*. Today we worship the ninth form of the Goddess, *Maa Siddhidatri*.

*🌟 Meaning of the name*
"Siddhi" means supernatural power/accomplishment and "Datri" means giver. She who grants every kind of siddhi is Siddhidatri.

*📖 The story*
According to the Devi Purana, Lord Shiva attained all siddhis through her grace. By her blessing, half of Shiva's body became the Goddess's, and he became known as *Ardhanarishvara*.

*✨ The eight siddhis*
Tradition counts eight major siddhis: Anima, Mahima, Garima, Laghima, Prapti, Prakamya, Ishitva and Vashitva. Maa Siddhidatri is believed to be the giver of all of them.

*🪷 Her form*
• Four arms, holding a mace, discus, conch and lotus
• Seated on a lotus
• Her vehicle is the lion

*🙏 Today's worship*
Today havan, Kanya Pujan and the concluding puja of Navratri are performed. Devotees end their nine days of devotion by thanking the Goddess.

*🍲 Bhog (offering)*
*Halwa, puri, chana* and *sesame (til)*.

*🕉️ Mantra*
_Om Devi Siddhidatryai Namah_

*✨ Message of the day*
Over nine days we remembered nine forms of strength, penance, courage, creation, motherhood, valour, fearlessness, purity and accomplishment. The real siddhi is to take at least one of these qualities into our lives and keep it going after the puja ends.

May Maa Siddhidatri fulfil all your heartfelt wishes. 🙏

*Jai Mata Di! 🚩 Happy Navratri to all!* 🌺

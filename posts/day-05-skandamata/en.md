🌺 *Navratri Day 5: Maa Skandamata* 🌺

_"Simhasanagata Nityam Padmashritakaradvaya,_
_Shubhadastu Sada Devi Skandamata Yashasvini."_

🙏 *Jai Mata Di!* On the fifth day of Navratri we worship the fifth form of the Goddess, *Maa Skandamata*.

*👶 Meaning of the name*
*Skanda* is another name of Lord Kartikeya. As the mother of Skanda, the Goddess is called Skandamata.

*📖 The story*
According to the Puranas, the demon Tarakasura had a boon that he could be slain only by a son of Shiva and Parvati. Goddess Parvati then gave birth to Kartikeya (Skanda) and he became commander of the gods' army. Skanda later slew Tarakasura. In this form the Goddess appears holding her son in her lap.

*🪷 Her form*
• Four arms: lotuses in two hands, infant Skanda in one, the other in the boon-granting gesture
• Seated on a lotus, so she is also called *"Padmasana"*
• Her vehicle is the lion
• Her complexion is pure white

*🧘 Spiritual significance*
It is believed her worship steadies meditation at the *Vishuddha Chakra*. The beautiful thing about this form is that along with her motherly love, she shares the honour of worship with her son. Devotees receive the blessings of both together.

*🍌 Bhog (offering)*
*Banana*. It is believed to keep the body healthy.

*🕉️ Mantra*
_Om Devi Skandamatayai Namah_

*✨ Message of the day*
A mother's love is selfless. Call your mother (or anyone who is like a mother to you) today and thank her. 🌸

May Maa Skandamata keep your family filled with love and protection. 🙏

*Jai Mata Di!* 🚩

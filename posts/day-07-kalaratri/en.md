🌺 *Navratri Day 7: Maa Kalaratri* 🌺

_"Ekaveni Japakarnapura Nagna Kharasthita,_
_Vamapadollasallohalatakantakabhushana."_

🙏 *Jai Mata Di!* The seventh day of Navratri belongs to the seventh form of the Goddess, *Maa Kalaratri*. She is considered the fiercest form, yet the most auspicious for devotees.

*🌑 Meaning of the name*
"Kala" means death/time and "Ratri" means night or darkness. She who destroys the darkness of ignorance and fear is Kalaratri.

*📖 The story*
The gods were troubled by the terror of Shumbha, Nishumbha and Raktabija. Raktabija had the power that every drop of his blood created a new Raktabija. The Goddess then took the form of Kalaratri and drank his blood before it could fall to the ground, bringing about his end. This story appears in the Durga Saptashati.

*🪷 Her form*
• Dark as deep night, with dishevelled hair
• A necklace that shines like lightning
• Three eyes, round like the universe
• Her vehicle is the donkey (Gardabha)
• A sword and an iron hook in two hands; the other two in the gestures of fearlessness and blessing

*💫 Why "Shubhankari"?*
Her form is fearsome, but she always gives auspicious results. This is why she is also called *Shubhankari*. Devotees have nothing to fear from her.

*🧘 Spiritual significance*
It is believed her worship opens the door of the *Sahasrara Chakra*. Fear, ghosts and negative forces are said to stay away.

*🍬 Bhog (offering)*
*Jaggery* or things made from it.

*🕉️ Mantra*
_Om Devi Kalaratryai Namah_

*✨ Message of the day*
Every night is followed by a dawn. Whatever fear or worry surrounds you, lay it at the Goddess's feet today. The deeper the darkness, the closer the hope of light.

May Maa Kalaratri remove the darkness of every fear from your life. 🙏

*Jai Mata Di!* 🚩

🌺 *Navratri Day 6: Maa Katyayani* 🌺

_"Chandrahasojjvalakara Shardulavaravahana,_
_Katyayani Shubham Dadyaddevi Danavaghatini."_

🙏 *Jai Mata Di!* The sixth day of Navratri is dedicated to the sixth form of the Goddess, *Maa Katyayani*, regarded as the warrior Goddess.

*⚔️ Meaning of the name*
Sage *Katyayana* performed intense penance and prayed that the Goddess be born as his daughter. She accepted, and so she is called Katyayani.

*📖 The story*
When the tyranny of Mahishasura grew, the Goddess emerged from the combined radiance of Brahma, Vishnu and Mahesh. Sage Katyayana was the first to worship her. She fought a fierce battle with Mahishasura and slew him, which is why she is known as *Mahishasuramardini*.

*🪷 Her form*
• Four arms: a sword in one, a lotus in another, the other two in the gestures of fearlessness and blessing
• Her vehicle is the lion
• A bright, golden complexion

*💍 Special belief*
The Shrimad Bhagavatam describes how the gopis of Braj worshipped Maa Katyayani on the banks of the Yamuna to have Shri Krishna as their husband. For this reason, tradition holds that she is worshipped to remove obstacles in marriage.

*🧘 Spiritual significance*
It is believed her worship brings focus to the *Ajna Chakra*, increasing confidence and decisiveness.

*🍯 Bhog (offering)*
*Honey*. It is believed to bring charm and sweetness.

*🕉️ Mantra*
_Om Devi Katyayanyai Namah_

*✨ Message of the day*
Do not stay silent in the face of injustice. Standing up for what is right is true worship. And remember, use strength to protect, not to show ego.

May Maa Katyayani remove every obstacle from your life. 🙏

*Jai Mata Di!* 🚩

🌺 *Navratri Day 3: Maa Chandraghanta* 🌺

_"Pindajapravararudha Chandakopastrakairyuta,_
_Prasadam Tanute Mahyam Chandraghanteti Vishruta."_

🙏 *Jai Mata Di!* The third day of Navratri is dedicated to the third form of Maa Durga, *Maa Chandraghanta*.

*🔔 Meaning of the name*
A *bell-shaped crescent moon* adorns her forehead, hence the name Chandraghanta ("chandra" = moon, "ghanta" = bell).

*📖 The story*
It is believed that after her marriage to Lord Shiva, Maa Parvati began wearing a crescent moon on her forehead and has been known as Chandraghanta since. According to the Devi Bhagavata and Puranic traditions, when the demons' terror grew, the Goddess protected the gods in this form. It is said that the sound of her bell alone makes evil forces tremble.

*🪷 Her form*
• A golden, radiant complexion
• *Ten arms* holding a trident, mace, sword, bow and arrow, lotus and other weapons
• Her vehicle is the lion
• Poised for battle, yet with a peaceful face

*🧘 Spiritual significance*
It is believed her worship activates the *Manipura Chakra*, bringing courage, fearlessness and gentleness. This is the form that teaches both strength and peace.

*🥛 Bhog (offering)*
*Milk-based items* such as kheer or sweets.

*🕉️ Mantra*
_Om Devi Chandraghantayai Namah_

*✨ Message of the day*
True strength is not in shouting, but in inner calm and standing firm at the right moment. Be both brave and peaceful, like Maa Chandraghanta.

May the Goddess remove every fear and obstacle from your life. 🙏

*Jai Mata Di!* 🚩

🌺 *Navratri Day 4: Maa Kushmanda* 🌺

_"Surasampurnakalasham Rudhiraplutameva Cha,_
_Dadhana Hastapadmabhyam Kushmanda Shubhadastu Me."_

🙏 *Jai Mata Di!* Today is the fourth day of Navratri, when we worship the fourth form of the Goddess, *Maa Kushmanda*.

*☀️ Meaning of the name*
"Ku" means a little, "ushma" means warmth/energy, and "anda" means cosmic egg or universe. It is believed that when there was only darkness everywhere, the Goddess created the universe with her *gentle smile*. She is therefore called the primal power of creation.

*📖 Belief*
She is said to reside *inside the Sun's orb*, and only she has the power to bear its intense heat. Her own radiance shines like the Sun.

*🪷 Her form*
• *Eight arms*, so she is also called "Ashtabhuja Devi"
• Holding a kamandalu, bow, arrow, lotus, pot of nectar, discus and mace
• The eighth hand holds a rosary that grants all siddhis and nidhis (powers and treasures)
• Her vehicle is the lion

*🧘 Spiritual significance*
It is believed that devotion to her awakens the *Anahata Chakra*. Illness, sorrow and suffering are said to fade, and life span, fame, strength and health increase.

*🍰 Bhog (offering)*
*Malpua*. It is believed to enhance intellect and decision-making.

*🕉️ Mantra*
_Om Devi Kushmandayai Namah_

*✨ Message of the day*
The Mother who created the whole universe with one smile reminds us how powerful positivity is. Today, smile sincerely and wish well to at least one person.

May Maa Kushmanda fill your life with health and energy. 🙏

*Jai Mata Di!* 🚩

🌺 *Navratri Day 8: Maa Mahagauri (Durga Ashtami)* 🌺

_"Shvete Vrishe Samarudha Shvetambaradhara Shuchih,_
_Mahagauri Shubham Dadyanmahadevapramodada."_

🙏 *Jai Mata Di!* Today is the eighth day of Navratri, known as *Durga Ashtami*. Today we worship the eighth form of the Goddess, *Maa Mahagauri*.

*🤍 Meaning of the name*
"Maha" means great and "Gauri" means fair-complexioned. Her radiance is compared to a conch, the moon and the jasmine (kunda) flower.

*📖 The story*
During her severe penance to win Lord Shiva, the Goddess's body became dark with dust and dirt. Pleased with her devotion, Shiva accepted her and washed her body with the waters of the Ganga. She then shone fair and radiant like lightning and became known as *Mahagauri*.

*🪷 Her form*
• White garments and white ornaments
• Four arms: a trident in one, a damaru in another, the other two in the gestures of fearlessness and blessing
• Her vehicle is the bull (Vrishabha)
• Age: she is believed to remain forever in the form of an eight-year-old girl

*🙏 Kanya Pujan*
Today is traditionally the day of *Kanya Pujan*. Nine girls (and one boy) are invited home, their feet are washed, a tilak is applied, and they are served halwa, puri and chana. They are regarded as living forms of the Goddess. Many families perform this on Navami instead. Follow your own family tradition.

*🧘 Spiritual significance*
It is believed that her worship washes away the sins of past births and brings purity of mind.

*🥥 Bhog (offering)*
*Coconut* or coconut-based sweets.

*🕉️ Mantra*
_Om Devi Mahagauryai Namah_

*✨ Message of the day*
On life's journey we all get soiled by worry, anger and envy. But through discipline, forgiveness and a sincere heart, we can become pure again.

May Maa Mahagauri fill your life with purity and light. 🙏

*Jai Mata Di!* 🚩

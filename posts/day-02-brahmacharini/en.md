🌺 *Navratri Day 2: Maa Brahmacharini* 🌺

_"Dadhana Karapadmabhyam Akshamala Kamandalu,_
_Devi Prasidatu Mayi Brahmacharinyanuttama."_

🙏 *Jai Mata Di!* On the second day of Navratri we worship the second form of Maa Durga, *Maa Brahmacharini*.

*📿 Meaning of the name*
"Brahma" means penance and "Charini" means one who practises it. She is the Goddess who practises austerity.

*📖 The story*
Parvati, born to Himalaya, performed severe penance on the advice of Devarshi Narada to win Lord Shiva as her husband.
• For thousands of years she lived only on fruits and flowers
• Then on dried bilva leaves
• Finally she gave up even the leaves, which is why she is also called *Aparna*

Her austerity shook the three worlds, and Lord Shiva finally accepted her.

*🪷 Her form*
• Dressed in white
• A rosary (japa mala) in the right hand, a kamandalu (water pot) in the left
• Calm, radiant and gentle

*🧘 Spiritual significance*
It is believed her worship awakens the *Svadhisthana Chakra*. Devotees grow in austerity, renunciation, detachment, good conduct and self-restraint.

*🍬 Bhog (offering)*
*Sugar / mishri* and panchamrit. It is believed this brings the blessing of long life to the family.

*🕉️ Mantra*
_Om Devi Brahmacharinyai Namah_

*✨ Message of the day*
Maa Brahmacharini teaches that however difficult the goal, it can be reached through steady effort and patience. When the path feels hard, remember her penance.

May the Goddess grant you success in every test and peace of mind. 🙏

*Jai Mata Di!* 🚩

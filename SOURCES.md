# Sources and Notes

## Primary textual sources

1. **Devi Kavacha** (part of the Durga Saptashati / Markandeya Purana): the verse naming the nine forms in order:
   *Prathamam Shailaputri cha, dvitiyam Brahmacharini; tritiyam Chandraghanteti, Kushmandeti chaturthakam; panchamam Skandamateti, shashtham Katyayaniti cha; saptamam Kalaratriti, Mahagauriti chashtamam; navamam Siddhidatri cha, Navadurgah prakirtitah.*
2. **Durga Saptashati / Devi Mahatmya** (Markandeya Purana, chapters 81-93): stories of Mahishasura, Shumbha-Nishumbha and Raktabija (used in Days 6 and 7).
3. **Other texts** with accounts of these forms: Devi Bhagavata Purana, Shiva Purana, Kalika Purana.
4. **Shrimad Bhagavatam**: the gopis' worship of Katyayani (Day 6).

## Notes

- The Devi Kavacha gives only the **names and order** of the nine forms. Detailed stories, bhog, chakra associations and iconography come mainly from the Puranas and living tradition, and vary by region.
- Day-wise bhog and chakra associations are traditional beliefs and are marked as such in the posts ("it is believed").
- The dhyana shlokas in each post should be verified against a printed edition before the posts go out.
